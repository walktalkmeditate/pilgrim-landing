I took a walk. <3

01Card 31 · Barnes & Noble

It's been a weird couple weeks for me. I just moved to the area for work and still adjusting to it. I took myself out for a walk just to get some fresh air and clear my head when I wandered into a bookstore. Browsing for books, one of them caught my eye. "On living and dying well" By Cicero. Feeling curious I grabbed it and opened it up for a quick read. Thats when I found it.

read on

Bookmarked on a page that could have been intentional. I started reading the chapter, the back of the book, reading about Cicero and the time period it was written in. It brought me back to learning about things that interest me. And the quote on the back of the card "take a walk" was hilarious to me because this was where my walk took me.

Noticing the URL I picked up my phone and searched it. Finding it was like magic. I felt like a kid with a treasure map. I couldn't pass up on the book so I bought it. Downloaded the app and reading the website. Thinking about my next walk.
