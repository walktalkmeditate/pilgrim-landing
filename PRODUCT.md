# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Mindfulness-curious walkers — people who want a calmer, more intentional relationship with walking, not step-counts, streaks, or fitness metrics. They arrive drawn to reflection and craft over performance. The site speaks to a prospective walker meeting Pilgrim for the first time and deciding whether this belongs in their life.

## Product Purpose

The landing page for Pilgrim, a privacy-first app for intentional walking as creative practice. It exists to introduce that practice and move the right people toward trying it. Success is people adopting the practice — installs — from visitors who feel the calm and the care before they ever open the app.

## Positioning

Walking itself is the practice — a crafted, quiet alternative to gamified fitness and busy meditation apps. Every screen reinforces that the walk is the point, not the metrics around it.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: download the app (App Store / Google Play). Secondary, for visitors not ready to install: explore an almanac instrument (`/sunpath`, `/moonpath`, `/daylight`) and feel the craft firsthand.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: *The walk is the practice.*
- Belief ladder: ① walking can be a practice, not just exercise → ② Pilgrim treats it with craft and calm, not gamification → ③ it respects my attention and privacy — on-device, nothing tracked → ④ it is beautiful and trustworthy enough to begin.
- Proof on hand: the press page and the App Store previews. No testimonials captured yet; add any to `.impeccable/assets/proof/` as they arrive.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, crafted, reverent; contemplative, poetic, seasonal. The voice is unhurried and literary — Cormorant Garamond, Bashō haiku, a real moon phase, color that shifts with the season and the time of day. It treats walking as close to sacred, and it never sells; it offers.

## Anti-references

Not gamified fitness apps (Strava, step-counters, rings) with their badges, streaks-as-competition, and performance pressure. Not loud SaaS landing pages with gradient heroes, big-number metric templates, and growth-hacky CTAs. Not busy meditation apps that are feature-listy and salesy — cluttered UI that sells serenity instead of offering it.

## Design Principles

1. The walk is the point — favor presence over destination, and never gamify.
2. Craft is the argument — show, don't tell; the real moon, the seasonal shifts, and the goshuin seal prove the care that words can't.
3. Quiet over loud — earn attention rather than grab it; restraint is the aesthetic.
4. Honesty over vanity — real numbers, no inflated metrics, privacy respected as a first principle.
5. The page is itself a practice — the experience embodies the product; the single scroll is a walk from threshold to horizon.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA, with `prefers-reduced-motion` honored throughout — the site already ships semantic HTML, reduced-motion alternatives, and ARIA labels. The seasonal color engine must preserve text contrast (body ≥ 4.5:1) across every season and in both light and dark modes; motion is enhancement, never a gate on content.
